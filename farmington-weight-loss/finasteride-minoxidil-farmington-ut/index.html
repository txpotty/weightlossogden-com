    How Finasteride + Minoxidil Works in Farmington, UT

    How Finasteride + Minoxidil Works in Farmington

    Finasteride blocks type 2 5-alpha reductase, the enzyme that turns testosterone into DHT. Lower DHT protects follicles from the slow miniaturization behind pattern loss. Minoxidil works on a separate track. It extends the growth phase of each hair and supports thicker strands, likely by improving blood flow and signaling at the follicle.

    Who This Combination Is For

    It is mainly for adult men with male pattern hair loss, such as a receding hairline or thinning crown. Early treatment keeps the most hair, since long-bald spots seldom respond. Some women past menopause use it off-label. Women who could become pregnant cannot.

    Dosing and Daily Schedule in Farmington

    Oral finasteride is FDA-approved at 1 mg once a day for male pattern hair loss. Minoxidil comes as a twice-daily topical or a low-dose off-label tablet. One spray daily also works. A compounded topical that combines finasteride and minoxidil goes on the scalp once a day.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Expect some shedding in months one and two. The early shed is common and usually temporary. By months three to six, most men see less loss and some thickening at the crown. In the original finasteride trials, most men kept their hair over five years, and many saw regrowth. Full results show near month twelve.

    Side Effects of Finasteride + Minoxidil in Farmington

    Finasteride may lower libido, affect erections or reduce ejaculate volume in about one to two percent of men. These usually resolve after stopping, and rare mood changes, including low mood, should be reported to your provider right away. Scalp dryness is the usual topical issue. The oral form sometimes causes fluid retention or a quicker pulse. Topical finasteride may lower whole-body exposure.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider asks about liver health, heart conditions, blood pressure, mood history and sexual health. They also review every medication and supplement, and they ask whether you plan to have children soon, since finasteride can slightly lower sperm count in some men. PSA results drop by about half. Women who could become pregnant should not handle crushed or broken tablets.

    How It Compares to Other Options in Farmington

    Finasteride + minoxidil is the standard first combination for men. When finasteride falls short, dutasteride is the usual next step, off-label for hair. Minoxidil + GHK-Cu is topical-only. Minoxidil alone helps, but it does nothing about DHT.
